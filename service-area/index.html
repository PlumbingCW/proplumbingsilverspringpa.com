A few of these towns sit in different townships and boroughs, so permit and sewer questions go to the local office in each one.

What changes from town to town

Columbia and Marietta are river boroughs on the east bank of the Susquehanna, so crews there often work on old lines that have been added to over generations. Mountville was incorporated as a borough in 1906 to set up a municipal water system, and Landisville is a census-designated place inside East Hempfield Township. Lancaster is a city with its own water department.

Water service differs too. Columbia Water Company says it serves Columbia, Mountville and Marietta plus West Hempfield and several other townships, and Lancaster City Water appears on the West Hempfield Township website as well. Some properties use a private well. Check your bill to learn which applies, and tell the dispatcher.

Which office to ask about permits

Permit rules vary by municipality, so ask the office that covers your address before work starts.

  • Silver Spring homes: West Hempfield Township, 3476 Marietta Avenue, Lancaster, PA 17601.
  • Landisville homes: East Hempfield Township.
  • Lancaster homes: the City of Lancaster, which has its own plumbing permit application.
  • Columbia, Mountville and Marietta homes: the borough office.
  • Not sure? Call the dispatcher, who can point you to the right office.

If your ZIP code is not on the list

Several other ZIP codes lie within about ten miles, including 17552 Mount Joy, 17545 Manheim, 17582 Washington Boro, 17551 Millersville and 17516 Conestoga. We do not promise coverage for every one, because it depends on the job and the schedule that day.

Call (717) 349-9373 and give your address. The dispatcher will say plainly whether a plumber can reach you, and if not, will say so right away so you can call someone else without losing time.

How to give your location on a call

The Silver Spring community has a ZIP that works like a P.O. box, so many residents have a Lancaster or Columbia mailing address even though they live in Silver Spring. Give the street name, the nearest main road such as PA Route 23, and the ZIP printed on your mail.

Landmarks help too. Lake Grubb Nature Park, Silver Spring Park on Ironville Pike and Hempfield Hill Road are all inside the area, and a dispatcher who knows them can place your address quickly. Ready now? Call (717) 349-9373 and start with your street name.
