Choosing between an emergency call and a planned visit

Ask one question: is something getting worse while you read this? Water that keeps spreading, sewage that keeps rising and a house with no heat in freezing weather are emergencies. A fixture that works poorly but safely is usually a planned visit.

Planned work still benefits from a call to the same number. The dispatcher can tell you what information helps the plumber arrive prepared, such as the age of the water heater or which floor the problem is on.
