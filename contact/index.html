Ways to reach us

Phone: (717) 349-9373, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays. Email: contact@proplumbingsilverspringpa.com for questions that can wait. Email is not monitored for emergencies, so never use it when water is spraying or a drain is backing up.

There is no walk-in address listed on this site, so phone the dispatcher instead of making a trip.

What to tell the dispatcher

A few details help the plumber arrive prepared.

  • Your street address, nearest main road and the ZIP printed on your mail.
  • What you see or hear: spraying water, a gurgling drain, no hot water or a bad smell.
  • Whether water is still running and whether you have found the main shutoff valve.
  • Whether the home uses public water and sewer or a private well and septic.
  • Any access issue, such as a locked gate, a basement door or a pet.

Emergencies and safety

If you smell gas, leave the building first, then call your gas utility or 911 from outside. For fire, injury or electrical danger near water, call 911. Call our line for the plumbing problem once everyone is safe.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you prefer not to be recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call.

Service hours

The line answers 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays. Same-day appointments are offered when available for work that is not an emergency.
