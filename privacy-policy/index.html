Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Silver Spring Pro Plumbing receives when you use this website or call our line, and how we use it. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Call (717) 349-9373All plumbing services

Information we receive

This website has no forms, accounts or sign-ups. We do not ask you to type personal details into the site. The information we receive comes from the ways you choose to contact us: phone calls and email.

When you call, we may receive your phone number, your name, your address and the details of the problem you describe. When you email, we receive your email address and the content of your message.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. A recording can include what you say and the number you called from. If you prefer not to be recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call.

How we use information

We use what you tell us to arrange plumbing service, to follow up on a visit and to improve how our dispatchers handle calls. We do not sell personal information.

We may share details with the plumber assigned to your job so they can reach you and find your address. We may also share information when the law requires it.

Third-party tools on this site

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and show maps from Google Maps. Those services may receive your IP address and browser details when a page loads, under Google's own privacy policy. We do not control what they collect.

If we add analytics or call tracking in the future, we will update this policy to name the tool and what it records.

Cookies

This site does not set its own cookies. Third-party services such as Google Maps may set cookies of their own. You can block cookies in your browser settings, though maps may behave differently.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it, subject to legal and business record requirements. Send requests to contact@proplumbingsilverspringpa.com.

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions can go to contact@proplumbingsilverspringpa.com or by mail to 1115 Lancaster Ave, Columbia, PA 17512.

Questions about your information?

Email us or call the line and ask for the person who handles privacy requests.
