Frozen Pipes: What to Do First and How to Prevent Them in Silver Spring, PA

If a pipe in your Silver Spring, PA home is frozen, open the faucet it feeds, find your main water shutoff, and warm the pipe slowly with a hair dryer on low. Never use a flame. If the pipe is cracked or water is spraying, shut the main off and call.

By the Silver Spring Pro Plumbing team. Published October 15, 2026. Updated October 15, 2026.

What to do first when a pipe freezes

Start with these steps in order. They work for a pipe that is frozen but not yet leaking.

  1. Find the main shutoff

    Know where it is before you touch anything else. If a pipe splits when it thaws, you want to close the valve fast.

  2. Open the faucet the frozen line feeds

    An open tap lets melting water and pressure escape as the ice clears.

  3. Locate the frozen section

    Look along exterior walls, in the garage, crawl space, under cabinets and near the basement ceiling. Frost on a pipe or a bulge is a clue.

  4. Warm it slowly

    Use a hair dryer on a low setting, a heating pad or warm towels. Start at the faucet end and work toward the frozen section.

  5. Check for leaks as the water returns

    Look along the whole length of the pipe. Any drip, spray or damp spot means shut the main off and call.

If a pipe has already burst or water is spraying

A split pipe floods quickly once the ice melts, and thawing can make it worse. Close the main shutoff right away, then call our 24/7 line. If water is near outlets or the panel, stay away and switch off power only if you can reach the panel while dry.

  • Close the main shutoff.
  • Open a low faucet to drain the lines.
  • Move belongings away from the water.
  • Photograph damage for your insurer.
  • Do not use an open flame to dry or thaw anything.

What not to do

Do not use a torch, a propane heater or a kerosene heater to thaw a pipe. An open flame can ignite framing and insulation, and fumes are dangerous in a closed space. Do not use a space heater near water or damp floors.

Do not keep thawing a pipe you can see is cracked. Do not ignore a frozen pipe in the hope it clears on its own, because pressure can build behind the ice. If you cannot find the frozen section, or it sits behind a finished wall, stop and call.

Where pipes freeze and how to protect them

The weakest points are the places where cold air reaches the pipe.

Prevention before the first cold snap

Most frozen pipe repairs start with a pipe that was never protected.

  • Disconnect garden hoses and shut off exterior spigots from the inside if you have a valve.
  • Insulate exposed pipes in the garage, crawl space and exterior walls.
  • Open cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls during a hard freeze.
  • Let a faucet drip slightly on the coldest nights if a line has frozen before.
  • Keep the thermostat at a steady setting even if you travel.
  • Label your main shutoff so anyone in the house can find it quickly.

Leaving the house in winter

Pipes freeze most often when a house sits cold. If you are away for several days, keep the heat on at a steady, moderate setting and ask a neighbor to look in. Some owners shut the main off and drain the lines, but that choice depends on your heating system, so ask a plumber first.

A burst pipe in an empty house can run for days before anyone notices. A leak alarm or an automatic shutoff valve is a reasonable add-on, and the main valve should be easy to reach and turn.

A frozen pipe may be a symptom, not the whole problem

A pipe that freezes once will freeze again if the cause is a draft, a missing insulation sleeve or a heating failure. Look for the cold source while you thaw. A pipe can also freeze and crack without leaking until it warms, so check the area an hour or two after water returns.

When the heat goes out too

A boiler shutting down in a cold snap can let pipes freeze quickly, and it can also be the result of a freeze. On some high-efficiency boilers, a frozen condensate drain can cause the unit to lock out. If your boiler stops, check for an error code and note it, but do not relight a pilot yourself.

Boiler repair is a job for a licensed plumber. If you smell gas, leave the building and call your gas utility or 911 from outside.

After the thaw: what the plumber looks for

After a freeze, a plumber who responds to a burst pipe repair will usually check the entire run, not just the split. Frozen pipes weaken beyond the visible crack, and a patch on one section may leave a second weak spot a few feet away.

Typical steps include shutting the water off, draining the line, cutting out the damaged section, installing new pipe and pressure testing before turning water back on. Coverage for damage varies by policy, so ask your insurer, and photograph everything first.

Older homes in the boroughs

As general trade knowledge, older homes in river boroughs like Marietta and in city neighborhoods near Lancaster often have pipes on exterior walls or in unheated basements. These layouts are more prone to freezing than newer interior routing. A walk through your own basement with a flashlight before winter tells you where your weak spots are.
